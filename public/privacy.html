Privacy policy

How GymKhata collects, uses and protects your information.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and phone number, and your sign-in details. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Gym records: member details, membership plans, fee payments, attendance and announcements that gym owners and staff enter in the app.
  • Subscription payments: payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive the payment status and reference, and we do not see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank details.
  • Location: only if your gym uses a printed QR code, the app checks your phone’s location once at check-in to confirm you are at the gym. We do not track you at other times.
  • Camera: used only to scan a gym’s QR code. Photos and video are not stored.
  • Notifications: a device token so we can send you push notifications from your gym.

How we use it

To run the app, show gym records to the right people, record check-ins and payments, send announcements and reminders, process subscriptions, keep the service secure, and answer your questions. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

Who else handles data

We use these services to run GymKhata: Supabase (database and sign-in), Razorpay (payments), Google (sign-in) and Netlify (website hosting). They process data only to provide their service to us. A gym’s records are visible to that gym’s owner and the staff the owner allows.

Keeping it safe

Access to each gym’s records is limited by role, and data is sent over secure connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in private.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep records while an account is active. You can delete your account inside the app, or email us to ask for your data to be corrected or deleted. We will respond within a reasonable time, and we may keep payment records where the law requires.

Children

GymKhata is not meant for children to use on their own. A member under 18 should be added by a parent, a guardian or the gym with a guardian’s consent.
