Run your gym without paper registers

GymKhata is a simple app for gym owners, staff and members. Fees, attendance, reminders and reports, all in one place.

Open the web appDownload the Android app

What you get

Fees and payments

Record cash and UPI payments, and see at a glance who has paid and who is due.

WhatsApp reminders

One tap opens the member’s WhatsApp chat with the fee reminder ready to send.

Easy attendance

Members check in with a tap, a live QR code, or a printed QR code at the gym. You choose which ways to allow.

Announcements

Post news to your members, and send push notifications through the Android app.

Branches and staff

Run many branches, and decide what each staff member can see and do.

Simple reports

See your members, collections and attendance without adding up a register.

Who uses it

  • Gym owners set up branches, plans and staff, follow the money, and manage the subscription.
  • Staff add members, record payments and take attendance, within the access the owner gives them.
  • Members join with an invite code from their gym, check in, and follow their gym’s announcements.

How it works

  1. Sign up as a gym owner on the web or in the Android app, and create your gym.
  2. Add your branches, plans and members, and invite your staff with a code.
  3. Collect fees, track attendance and send reminders from your phone.
