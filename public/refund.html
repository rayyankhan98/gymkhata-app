Cancellation and refunds

Please read this before you pay for a subscription.

Cancelling

A subscription does not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. If you do not renew, the app pauses for your gym when the paid period ends. Until then you can keep using it.

Refunds

Subscription fees are not refundable once paid, because the service starts as soon as payment is confirmed.

We will refund you if you were charged twice for the same subscription, or if your payment went through but the subscription did not start.

How to ask for a refund

Email rayyankhan38017@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 7382962612. Please include your gym name, the email you signed up with, and the Razorpay payment ID if you have it.

How long it takes

An approved refund goes back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.

Gym membership fees paid by members to their gym are between the member and that gym, so ask the gym about those.
