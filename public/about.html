About us

GymKhata: Fees & Members is a gym management app.

GymKhata helps gym owners, staff and members run a gym without paper registers. It works for one gym or many, and for one branch or many.

What the app does

  • Keeps member records and membership plans for each branch.
  • Records fee payments made in cash or by UPI, and shows who has paid and who is due.
  • Lets members check in with a tap, a live QR code, or a printed QR code at the gym.
  • Sends announcements and fee reminders, including a button that opens the member’s WhatsApp chat.
  • Gives owners reports, and lets them control what staff can see and do.

Who runs it

GymKhata is run by Humera Sultana from Saidabad, Hyderabad, Telangana, India. Gym owners pay a monthly subscription for each branch, as set out on the pricing page.
